# SolarEPC Pro — Project Charter

## Purpose

SolarEPC Pro is a commercial & utility-scale solar EPC operating system. It supports EPCs from tender → design → pricing → execution → handover.

## Primary Users

- EPC Project Managers  
- Solar Engineers (PV \+ BESS)  
- Commercial & Utility Developers  
- Executives / Directors

## Core Capabilities

- Tender & preconditions management  
- EPC go/no-go evaluation  
- AI-assisted engineering & pricing  
- Project lifecycle tracking  
- Documentation & compliance outputs

## Non-Goals

- Residential solar  
- DIY installers  
- Consumer-facing portals

## Guiding Principles

- Engineering-grade accuracy  
- Bankable, conservative assumptions  
- Modular architecture  
- Minimal magic, maximum traceability
